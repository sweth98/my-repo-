# Dharmakshetra — The Chariot of Partha

A small browser game set on the battlefield of Kurukshetra (the Mahabharata).
You are **Arjuna**; **Krishna** holds the reins. Everything — the Pattachitra‑style
painting, the raga‑scale music and sound effects — is drawn and synthesised in code,
with no external assets.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any modern browser (no build step).

## How to play
| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Hold mouse / touch, release | Draw and loose the *Gandiva*. Aim at a target; hold longer to reach further. |
| `Space` / `↓` / on-screen *कृष्ण* | Krishna lowers the chariot (dodges the Nagastra) |
| `Q` / `E` / on-screen *अस्त्र* | Divine astra, once the meter is full — clears the field, but spares those who surrender |
| `M` | Mute |

- **Kavacha** is your armour. **Dharma** is your righteousness: strike an unarmed,
  surrendering warrior and it falls; let him pass and it is restored. At zero, the game ends.
- Four days of war — Bhishma's host, Drona's formation, the war elephants — and finally
  **Karna** on Day Seventeen, with his serpent‑arrow and his sinking wheel. What you do
  while he is unarmed decides the ending.
- Verses from the *Bhagavad Gita* (2.47, 2.23, 4.7, 18.78) close each day.
