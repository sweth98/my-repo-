# CanPlus.io: one-week B2B sales pipeline (24–28 Nov 2026)

Goal: one booked client by end of week. "Booked" means a signed paid pilot, LOI or PO, not a verbal yes.
Honest odds: enterprise and university LMS deals usually take 3–9 months. A one-week close needs warm contacts and a pilot small enough that one person can approve it. The plan is built around that.

## 1. What we are selling (from Clay / LinkedIn company profile)
CanPlus is a Canvas-open-source-based LMS that runs on the customer's own servers or private cloud, with:
- 100% data ownership (nothing shared with other tenants)
- AI course builder and "AI Buddy" (quizzes, flashcards) that run inside the customer's network
- Compliance (GDPR, FERPA) and full customization (internal integrations, simulators)

## 5. Engagement playbook (natural, non-spammy)
Principle: lead with relevance and a useful insight. Pitch only after they reply. 3–5 touches over 5 days, across LinkedIn and email.

**Day 0 – Warm-up (before any message)**
- Follow the person and the company. Like or comment thoughtfully on 1–2 recent posts. Do this 24–48h before the connection request.

**Touch 1 – Connection request (no pitch, <300 chars)**
> Hi {{first}}, I saw {{company}}'s work on {{specific initiative}}. I work on private-deployment learning platforms and follow how institutions are handling data control with AI. Would be glad to connect.

**Touch 2 – After accept (value first)**
> Thanks for connecting, {{first}}. Quick thought: many institutions want AI course tools but can't send student data to public AI. We built CanPlus (Canvas-based LMS that runs on your own servers, with the AI running inside your network). If it's useful, I can share a 2-page note on how teams handle FERPA/GDPR/DPDP with in-network AI. No pitch.

**Touch 3 – Email, day 2**
Subject: `{{company}} + AI inside your firewall?`
> Hi {{first}},
> I noticed {{trigger: hiring for X / new online programme / new campus / AI announcement}}. Teams in your position often hit the same wall: AI course-building tools are useful, but student and training data can't leave the network.
> CanPlus is a Canvas-based LMS deployed on your own servers or private cloud. AI course builder and study assistant run entirely inside your network. 100% data ownership.
> I'd like to offer a **30-day private pilot** (fee credited toward any annual contract) for one department or one course, set up by our team. Worth a 20-minute look this week?

**Touch 4 – Day 3, LinkedIn voice note or short Loom (60s)**
Walk through the AI course builder on a sample course. Personalize the first 5 seconds with their name and institution.

**Touch 5 – Day 4–5, breakup / referral ask**
> Totally understand if the timing isn't right. Would you know who handles digital learning or IT security at {{company}}? Happy to send them the 2-pager instead.

**Warm paths to try first (highest probability this week)**
1. Your own network: past colleagues, alumni, professors, former clients. Post a 1-paragraph LinkedIn post ("looking for 2 institutions to pilot an in-network AI LMS") and DM everyone who reacts.
2. Mutual connections on each target account (2nd-degree). Ask for a 2-line intro.
3. Canvas / Instructure user groups, IndiaLMS or edtech communities, local principals' associations: reply to real questions, don't post pitches.
4. Partners: ask ed-tech firms (Tier B) about white-label for their private clients.

## 9. Objection handling
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already use Canvas / Moodle / Blackboard" | CanPlus is Canvas-based, so migration is light. Add the AI + private hosting without retraining faculty. |
| "Is a small vendor safe?" | Open-source core, you hold the servers and the code. No lock-in. Escrow/source access available (confirm with your team). |
| "Who maintains it?" | We do SLA support; your IT keeps control. |
| "Need a tender / committee" | Use the pilot: department-level, under the procurement threshold. |
| "Budget" | Pilot fee is credited against the annual contract. |
