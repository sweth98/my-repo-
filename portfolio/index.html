About

A junior designer who ships, not just sketches.

I'm a Junior UI/UX Designer based in Hyderabad, with hands-on experience across UI design, UX design, wireframing, prototyping and design systems for web and mobile products. My process starts with mapping user flows and information architecture in Miro, then moves into high-fidelity Figma prototypes — so every screen traces back to a decision about what the user needs to do next.

What sets my process apart is where I sit between traditional UX fundamentals and AI-assisted design workflows: I use AI tools for rapid ideation, AI-generated visual and video assets, and prompt engineering to move from concept to prototype faster, while still grounding every interface in usability and accessibility basics like WCAG 2.1 and responsive behaviour.

Day to day, I work cross-functionally with marketing and development teams — designing UI mockups, campaign creatives and product visuals that stay on-brand and easy to build. Before design, I spent time in delivery and recruitment coordination, which is where the structured, stakeholder-facing side of my process comes from.
