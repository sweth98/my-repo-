# CanPlus sprint: daily checklist (Tue 24 - Sat 28 Nov 2026)

Goal: one signed paid pilot, LOI or PO. Source: `canplus-sales-pipeline.md` (sections 5-8). Log every action in `tracker.csv` as it happens, not at end of day.
Times are IST and are a suggested rhythm. Shift blocks, but keep the order. Nothing is sent without the founder approving the copy.

Funnel assumption (from plan section 7): about 75 touches -> 10-15 replies -> 4-5 demos -> 2 proposals -> 1 close. A "touch" is any outbound message (DM, connection request, email, voice note). If a stage runs below target, add volume at the stage above it the next morning.
