## 3. BITS Pilani
**Current LMS:** Taxila (BITS in-house name) via the eLearn portal (elearn.bits-pilani.ac.in); BITS e-services lists separate on-campus and off-campus LMS. Live sessions via Impartus. Underlying software (Moodle/other) UNVERIFIED; search results did not name it. Some sources are dated 2018.

**Recent signals**
- WILP announced new AI/ML and cybersecurity programmes on 28 Jan 2026 (first batch April 2026); MBA in AI for Business, April 2026 batch.
- CLAIMED ransomware attack: threat-intel aggregators list bits-pilani.ac.in as a victim of the DragonForce group, about 20-22 Jun 2026, with 61.88 GB leak claimed. This is the attacker's and aggregators' claim; no BITS confirmation found. UNVERIFIED.
- BITS's own data privacy policy cites the DPDP Act 2023 and breach reporting.
- No leadership change found (the search did not target it well; UNVERIFIED either way).

**Likely pain:** Large WILP base of working professionals (corporate partners, per WILP pages) whose data sits on a legacy-looking LMS; a possible security incident. Whether the incident is real is not established.

**Hook (use carefully):** Lead with the verified items only: new AI/ML and cybersecurity programmes (Jan 2026) and BITS's published DPDP-aligned privacy policy. "Your WILP AI programmes teach MLOps and GenAI; in-network AI for learners keeps student data inside your own perimeter." Do NOT mention the claimed breach in outreach. It is unconfirmed and a cold reference would read as opportunistic and could be wrong.

**Fit: 3/5.** Strong data-sovereignty motive and a big online base, and it is a private institute. But it has its own in-house LMS and the buyer would be the IT/security leadership, who are likely busy with any incident response, and the cycle is long.
