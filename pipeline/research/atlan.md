She also said the first 10 customers must be real market customers, not people the founders know, and that the team avoided using investor introductions. Source: https://sajithpai.com/pmf-convo-10-prukalpa-sankar-atlan/ (date not shown; the PMF Convo interview predates the 2024 raise, UNVERIFIED).
- **US first.** 60%+ of clients were in the US by 2021 (TechCrunch link above). Sankar discussed building a US "execution machine" (Datacast, https://datacast.substack.com/p/episode-76-modern-data-collaboration-ab9, a summary only; details UNVERIFIED).

## 6. Distinctive tactics
1. Be first or best inside a big platform's ecosystem (first Snowflake Ready catalog, Partner of the Year), then borrow its sales reach.
2. Re-name the category as the buyer's concern changes (collaboration, control plane, context layer).
3. Convert analyst reports into sales assets (a hub plus press releases).
4. Run virtual events and debates that carry opinion, not product demos.
5. Make the technical POC fast, with the testimonial claiming minutes.
6. Raise from investors who are also distribution (Salesforce Ventures, Snowflake alumni).

## Lessons for a small India-based B2B vendor selling a private on-prem LMS with in-network AI to universities and corporate L&D
1. **Pick one anchor ecosystem and earn a credential in it.** Atlan's Snowflake validation did the trust work abroad. An equivalent for you would be a listing or certification with an LMS-standards body (for example LTI or SCORM compatibility) or with a big directory or integrator, not just your own claims. You cannot copy a Partner of the Year award at your size.
2. **Make the POC fast and on the buyer's own infrastructure.** Atlan's POC claim is quick connection to the customer's stack. For on-prem, ship a reproducible install (container or VM image) and a time-boxed pilot, for example one department or one course, with success metrics agreed up front.
3. **Sell the privacy and in-network AI as the wedge, and name the buyer's current worry in their words.** Atlan changed its category label as buyer anxiety changed. For you the current worry is data leaving the campus or company to a public AI. Put that in headlines, but prove it with architecture diagrams and an audit-friendly data-flow document.
4. **Use founder-led, inbound-style selling at first, with honest early customers.** Sankar's advice was that the first 10 customers should be real market customers, not friends. Do the reverse of vanity: publish a small number of named, consented references (even Indian universities) and a candid pilot write-up. Atlan's 2021 US share of 60%+ shows global buyers can be won early, but it took funding and US hiring, which you should not assume you can match.
5. **Content and events: do a few opinionated, cheap things, and do not expect analyst recognition soon.** Atlan's Gartner and Forrester standing came after $200M+ of funding and years of scale. Copy the cheap parts: one recurring virtual panel for L&D and academic leaders, a knowledge base answering procurement and IT-security questions, and public pricing ranges or at least a pricing logic (per learner, per site) so buyers can self-qualify. Atlan's pricing is opaque and reviewers complain about it, so transparency is a possible edge for a small vendor, though the cost is less negotiating room.
