# What six fast-growing B2B companies teach CanPlus (synthesis, as of 24 Nov 2026)

Source: six research files in this folder (clay, ramp, harvey, magicschool, deel, atlan). They are built from web-search summaries only; the researchers did not open most pages. Many numbers are company-reported or third-party estimates and are marked UNVERIFIED in the individual files. Treat this as directional, not audited. Survivorship bias applies: we only study the winners.

## 2. Patterns that repeat (what actually shows up across companies)
1. **A narrow wedge, not "an LMS".** Harvey = legal AI; MagicSchool = teacher tools; Atlan = governance. CanPlus wedge: **private, in-network AI for learning**.
2. **A named early partner.** Harvey's Allen & Overy, MagicSchool's districts. CanPlus needs one named reference (with permission) from a scoped pilot.
3. **A free or low-risk way in.** Clay/Ramp/MagicSchool use free tiers. CanPlus can't give away hosted software, so the equivalent is a **sandbox, recorded demo or small paid pilot with a credited fee**.
4. **Trust documents before sales calls.** Harvey and MagicSchool publish security/privacy pages and sign data addenda. CanPlus needs a trust page, a data-handling FAQ, DPA, SSO notes, accessibility statement (VPAT). Never claim certifications you don't hold.
5. **Pilot → expand.** Harvey's seat growth, Deel's land-and-expand. Plan the pilot as the first step of a larger rollout, with written success criteria.
6. **Founder-led selling early.** Harvey (founder LinkedIn outreach), Ramp (founder customer calls), Atlan (founder-led, inbound). You should be doing the first 20 conversations yourself.
7. **Channels through trusted intermediaries.** Ramp's accountants, Harvey's PwC, Atlan's Snowflake, Clay's agencies. CanPlus: implementation partners, edtech consultants, system integrators, university IT consultants.
8. **Community and credible content.** Clay's community, Atlan's events and analyst hub, MagicSchool's ambassadors. CanPlus: webinars and checklists on AI + data sovereignty in education.

## 3. What does NOT transfer to CanPlus (and why)
- **Free self-serve signups at scale (Clay, MagicSchool, Ramp):** your product is deployed on the customer's own infrastructure. Use a gated sandbox or pilot instead.
- **High-volume automated cold outreach (Ramp):** at your stage, a small, personal, relevant list works better, and bulk email carries legal and reputation risk.
- **Deel's aggressive tactics:** the researcher reported allegations (reported lawsuits and a reported criminal probe, unresolved or unverified). Do not copy any competitor-intelligence shortcuts or inflated metrics.
- **Big analyst/marketplace plays (Atlan):** useful long-term; not a this-week lever.
- **Headline numbers:** treat as marketing. Do not quote them to prospects.

## 4. Gaps the research could not fill (be honest about these)

- On-premises or private deployment examples: none found among the six.
- Original ARR/customer figures: mostly company-reported or third-party estimates.

## 5. What to do with this at CanPlus, this week
1. **Pick one wedge sentence:** "Canvas-grade LMS with AI that runs inside your network." Use it everywhere.
2. **Build the trust pack (1–2 days):** one-page overview, data-handling FAQ (answers confirmed), data-processing terms, SSO/security notes, accessibility statement. Only state what's true.
3. **Win one named design partner:** offer one warm contact a scoped 30-day pilot at a friendly price in exchange for a reference and feedback. Make it easy to say yes.
4. **Founder-led outreach to your warm network first** (Ramp, Harvey pattern), 10–15 personal messages a day, then referrals.
5. **Offer a sandbox/reverse demo:** let them describe their data-and-AI concern first, then show the relevant screens (Clay's "reverse demo" idea).
6. **Recruit 2–3 intermediaries:** edtech consultants, Canvas implementation firms, IT system integrators. Offer a referral arrangement [decide terms].
7. **Publish one useful asset:** "5 questions to ask any LMS vendor about AI and your data." Post it on LinkedIn, send it to prospects.
8. **Define pilot success criteria in writing** and a day-21 review, so pilots turn into annual contracts (Harvey pattern).
