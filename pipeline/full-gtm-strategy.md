## PART 1: Foundation (do this first, 1 day)

### 1.1 Positioning
- **Category:** "Private LMS with in-network AI." Not "another LMS".
- **Promise:** Canvas-grade learning, AI included, and your data never leaves your walls.
- **Three proof points to build:** (1) a live demo where the AI demonstrably runs in-network, (2) a one-page security/data note, (3) your first named pilot (with permission to cite).
- **Who you are, honestly:** small, fast, founder-led vendor. Use it as a strength (direct access, custom work) not something to hide.

## PART 3: Channels: how to reach them

### 3.1 LinkedIn (primary channel)
**Profile setup (founder):**
- Headline: "Helping universities & enterprises run learning + AI on their own servers | CanPlus" (not a job title).
- About: problem → solution → who it's for → how to reach you. No jargon.
- Featured: demo video, one-pager, pilot request link.
- Company page: same story, pinned demo post.

**Daily routine (45–60 min/day):**
1. 10 min: comment thoughtfully on 5–10 posts by target buyers (L&D, IT, education leaders).
2. 15 min: 15–20 personalised connection requests (stay under ~100/week to avoid limits).
3. 15 min: message new connections with value-first note (no pitch).
4. 10 min: follow-ups, voice notes.
5. 5 min: log in tracker.

**Content (3×/week, 20 min each):**
- *Insight:* "Why AI tools in education raise data-residency questions" (no hype, a clear checklist).
- *How-to:* "5 questions to ask any LMS vendor about AI and your data."
- *Proof/progress:* short demo clips, pilot learnings (with permission), build-in-public updates.
- *Poll:* "Where does your LMS's AI process your data?" (generates leads from voters).
Rules: no fake stats, no unverifiable claims, no naming prospects without permission.

**Outreach sequence (see `pipeline/outreach-copy.md` for copy):** follow/comment → connect (no pitch) → value note → short voice note → soft ask for 15 min → referral ask.

**Sales Navigator (if you have it):** save leads by segment, use "Posted on LinkedIn" and "Changed jobs in last 90 days" filters. New heads of IT/L&D are most open to change.

**Groups and communities:** join and give answers in edtech, L&D, CIO, higher-ed IT, and Canvas/Instructure user communities. Answer for 2 weeks before ever mentioning CanPlus.

### 3.2 Email
**Setup:** separate sending domain; warm up 2–3 weeks if volume is high; 30–50/day per mailbox max; plain text, no tracking pixels at the start; include a real signature and an unsubscribe line.
**Compliance:** India DPDP and GDPR/UK PECR (B2B rules differ by country), CAN-SPAM. Use business emails, state who you are and why you're writing, honour opt-outs immediately. **[Check with a lawyer for the EU/UK.]**
**Sequence (5 emails over ~14 days):**
1. Day 1: relevant trigger + one-line offer + low-friction ask.
2. Day 3: 2-page note on in-network AI + data sovereignty.
3. Day 6: 90-second demo video link.
4. Day 10: pilot terms in 3 bullets.
5. Day 14: breakup + referral ask.
**Benchmarks to track:** open rate (if measured) 40–60%, reply rate 3–8% cold, 20–40% warm. Test 2 subject lines per segment.
**Sourcing emails:** Vibe Prospecting (needs credits), Clay enrichment, institution websites (staff directories), conference speaker lists. Do not buy random lists.

### 3.3 Phone and WhatsApp
- **Phone:** warm calls to people who replied, opened emails or accepted LinkedIn requests. Best for owners of training institutes and Indian private colleges.
- **WhatsApp:** only after the person has engaged or given a number. Short, no spam, send demo link.
- Script: "I'm [name] from CanPlus. I sent a note about running an LMS with AI on your own servers. Is data control for AI something you're looking at this year?"

### 3.4 Warm network and referrals (highest conversion)
- Message every relevant first-degree contact personally (see `pipeline/warm-network-plan.md`).
- Ask every contact: "Who else should I speak to?" (aim: 2 intros per call).
- Offer a referral thank-you (a gift, or a commission for partners) **[CONFIRM terms]**.
- Alumni networks, former colleagues, professors, vendor friends.

### 3.5 Events and webinars
- **Own webinar (monthly):** "Running AI in education without sending data to the cloud." 30 min + Q&A. Record it. Collect registrations → follow up in 24h.
- **Joint webinars** with a partner, association or a friendly university.
- **Conferences (India):** higher-ed and L&D events, edtech summits, university IT forums. Go as an attendee first; ask to speak on data sovereignty. Book 5 meetings before you arrive.
- **Roundtables:** invite 6–8 CIOs/L&D heads for a closed discussion on AI and data. Hosts earn trust.

### 3.6 Content and SEO
- Landing pages per segment: "Private LMS for universities", "On-premise LMS for corporate compliance training", "Canvas-based LMS with private AI".
- Articles: Canvas on-prem vs cloud; DPDP/GDPR/FERPA and your LMS; AI in education without public APIs; total cost of ownership; migration guides from Moodle/Blackboard (only after you've confirmed support).
- Comparison pages (honest, factual): CanPlus vs self-hosted Canvas vs SaaS LMS.
- Lead magnet: "AI & Data Checklist for your LMS vendor" (PDF), gated by email.
- Keep every claim verifiable.

### 3.7 Partnerships and resellers
- **Ed-tech/L&D firms** (Great Learning, NIIT MTS, LearningMate type): private-deployment white-label for their enterprise clients.
- **System integrators and IT consultancies** that bid for university/government IT: refer or co-bid.
- **Cloud and hosting providers**, **Canvas implementation consultants**: referral loop.
- **Associations** (university IT, L&D, HR bodies): sponsored webinar, member discount for pilots.
- Offer partners: margin, co-branding, technical enablement, joint pilots.

### 3.8 Public sector and tenders
- Register on government marketplaces where you sell (e.g. GeM in India) and watch e-procurement portals for LMS tenders. **[CONFIRM eligibility, startup exemptions, and required certifications.]**
- Use small-value pilots and PoCs; build references first, bid on tenders later.

### 3.9 Paid and light-touch ads (small test, month 2)
- LinkedIn ads targeted by job title (CIO, Head of L&D, Dean) at a small budget (₹/$ amount your call) pointing to the webinar or checklist, not a "buy now".
- Retarget website visitors. Measure cost per qualified meeting, not clicks.

### 3.10 Product-led and community hooks
- A public "sandbox" demo (sign-up link) with sample data. Gate real use behind a pilot request.
- Open-source and Canvas community contributions (docs, plugins) build credibility with technical buyers.
- Student/faculty champions: let a professor or trainer try it first. Champions pull in budget owners.

## PART 4: The sales process (stages and exit criteria)

| Stage | Exit criterion | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Target | Right person identified, trigger noted | Research 5 min (use `account-research.md`) |
| 2. Engaged | Replied / accepted / attended webinar | Book discovery |
| 3. Discovery done | Problem, owner, budget path, timeline known | Qualify (3 below) |
| 4. Demo | Seen demo tailored to their pain | Send recap in 2h |
| 5. Pilot proposal | Scope, users, success criteria, price, dates | Send order form |
| 6. Security/IT review | Questionnaire answered | Respond in 24h |
| 7. Signed | Order form + data terms signed | Kick-off date set |
| 8. Pilot live | Users onboarded | Weekly check-ins |
| 9. Conversion | Success criteria met → annual contract | Present results meeting at day 21 |

**Qualification (3 questions):** Is there a real problem (data, AI, cost, control)? Is there a person who can approve a small pilot? Is there a date by which they want to decide?

**Pilot success plan (agree before starting):** 3 measurable criteria (e.g. 70% of invited users active weekly, 5 AI-built courses published, IT sign-off on deployment/security), review meeting at day 21, decision at day 30.

**Closing tactics (honest):**
- Make the next step specific and dated.
- Offer a setup slot as the real constraint ("we do two pilot setups per month" only if true).
- Remove friction: pre-fill the order form, offer a call with their legal/IT team.
- Credit the pilot fee toward the annual contract.
- Ask: "What would make this a clear yes?"

**Objection handling:** see `pipeline/collateral.md` and the pitch guide: existing LMS, small vendor, committee/tender, security, budget, AI specifics.

## PART 6: Operating rhythm and numbers

**Weekly cadence (founder):**
- Mon: pipeline review, set targets; send new batch.
- Tue–Thu: outreach blocks (morning), calls/demos (afternoon), content (1 post).
- Fri: follow-ups, webinar/partner calls, update tracker and metrics, plan next week.

**Funnel math (planning guide, adjust with real data):**
75 touches → 10–15 replies → 4–5 demos → 2 pilot proposals → 1 pilot signed.
For 5 pilots in 90 days, you need ~375 well-targeted touches, ~25 demos, ~10 proposals.

**KPIs (weekly):** touches, replies, meetings booked, demos, proposals, pilots signed, pilot→annual conversion, cycle time, reply rate by segment/message.
**Review rule:** after 100 touches per segment, drop the lowest-performing segment/message and double down on the best.

**CRM:** start with `tracker.csv` or a free CRM. HubSpot/Close/Apollo connectors failed to connect in this session, so use a spreadsheet or Notion until they work.
